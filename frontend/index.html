Know the experiment

Community-funded, with no creator fees or reserved NFTs. Mining is open: GPUs and mining farms can participate. More computing power can find more proofs.

Keep it or burn it

A living cat accumulates ETH paid by later miners. After the pool launches, its owner can permanently burn it to receive its accumulated ETH and BCAT together. ETH cannot be withdrawn while keeping the cat. Rewards start at 1,000 BCAT and decrease by 5% every 512 total mints, using the epoch at burn time.

Art, not a yield promise

Common 60% · Rare 25% · Epic 12% · Legendary 3% are generation weights, not guaranteed collection counts. Extra computation can select cosmetic traits. Every cat has a unique serial pattern. Rarity does not affect rewards.

No new mints means no new rent from mints. A buyback does not guarantee a price increase. Funds cannot be refunded if the community never reaches launch. This experimental software has not had an independent audit.
